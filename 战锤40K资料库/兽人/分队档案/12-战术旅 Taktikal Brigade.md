## 分队规则（团策）
### Lissen 'Ere 听好了
【原】Friendly STORMBOYZ units have BATTLELINE. When a friendly BOYZ / KOMMANDOS / STORMBOYZ unit is selected to make an advance / fall-back move, that move does not prevent that unit from being eligible to start an action.

中文速查：
- 友军 STORMBOYZ（暴风小子）单位获得 BATTLELINE 关键词
- BOYZ/KOMMANDOS/STORMBOYZ单位推进或撤退移动时，不阻止该单位开始执行行动（Action）——做任务类行动还能全速机动
