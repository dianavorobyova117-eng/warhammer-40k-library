## 分队规则（团策）
### Thundering Wagons 雷鸣战车
【原】Friendly BATTLEWAGON / HUNTA RIG / KILL RIG units have WAGON. Friendly WAGON units can re-roll charge rolls. When a friendly WAGON unit is selected to make an advance move, that unit can change advance rolls to a 6.
【原】This detachment has the WAGONS tag and cannot be taken with another WAGONS detachment.

中文速查：
- 友军 BATTLEWAGON/HUNTA RIG/KILL RIG 单位获得 WAGON 关键词
