# 极速哇啊！ Speedwaaagh!（载具狂飙流）
**DP消耗：2** ｜ 来源：Wahapedia 11ed（Faction Pack: Orks v1.2, 2026-08）

## 分队规则（团策）
### Turbo Boostas 涡轮推进器
【原】Each time a SPEED FREEKS or TRUKK unit (excluding AIRCRAFT) from your army Advances, it can use its turbo. If it does, do not make an Advance roll for it; instead, until the end of the phase, models in that unit have a Move characteristic of 24" but can only move in one straight line, and until the end of the turn, ranged weapons equipped by those models have the [ASSAULT] ability and that unit cannot declare a charge.

中文速查：
- 飙车爽仔/TRUKK单位推进时可用"涡轮"：不掷推进骰，当阶段移速固定24"，只能直线移动（设计注：不能转弯、不能绕障碍）
- 涡轮后当回合远程武器获得[ASSAULT]，但不可宣布冲锋（即使别的规则允许推进后冲锋也不行）
- 24"直线冲刺=一回合横穿半场的斩首/占点工具
