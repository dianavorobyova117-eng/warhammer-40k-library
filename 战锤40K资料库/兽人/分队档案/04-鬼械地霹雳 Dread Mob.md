## 分队规则（团策）
### Try Dat Button! 试试那颗按钮！
【原】Each time a MEK, ORKS WALKER or GROTS VEHICLE unit from your army is selected to shoot or fight, roll one D6. Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in that unit have the corresponding ability shown in the table below.（D6按钮表：1-2=[SUSTAINED HITS 1]；3-4=[LETHAL HITS]；5-6=Critical Wound时AP+2）/ Alternatively... you can select one of the abilities above instead of rolling the D6. If you do... weapons... have the [HAZARDOUS] ability as well. If a weapon... has the [HAZARDOUS] ability from multiple sources, each time you take a Hazardous test for that weapon, it is failed on a roll of a 1-3.（v1.2勘误后文本）

中文速查：
- 每次MEK/兽人步行者（ORKS WALKER）/葛洛茨载具单位射击或挥砍时掷D6：1-2全体武器[SUSTAINED HITS 1]；3-4[LETHAL HITS]；5-6暴击伤时AP+2
- 可改为自选效果，代价是全体武器附加[HAZARDOUS]（危险物，1时携带者受致命伤）；多重HAZARDOUS时1-3都算失败
- 赌按钮还是花钱买稳定，每回合的心理博弈

### KEYWORDS
- **GRETCHIN单位获得 BATTLELINE 关键词**（葛洛茨变战线单位——组军结构性质变化）

## 组建限制（若有）

## 战术画像
- 赌狗团策：D6按钮随机三选一，自选则吃HAZARDOUS——高方差高上限，Press It Fasta（35分）双效果是核心解
- 四个战略都有"加压"选项：加伤害/加命中但武器变HAZARDOUS，与团策的代价机制一体成型
- 死亡机甲群：Deff Dreads、Killa Kans、Gorkanaut/Morkanaut（步行者）+ Mek Gunz（葛洛茨载具）
- GRETCHIN战列化让小绿皮海填满战列位，机甲填重槽——结构上天然混合军
