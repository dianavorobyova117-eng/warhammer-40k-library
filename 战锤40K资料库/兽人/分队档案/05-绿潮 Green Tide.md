# 绿潮 Green Tide
**DP消耗：3** ｜ 来源：Wahapedia 11ed（Faction Pack: Orks v1.2, 2026-08）

## 分队规则（团策）
### Mob Mentality 暴徒心态
【原】Each time an attack targets a BOYZ unit from your army, models in that unit have a 6+ invulnerable save against that attack. Each time an attack targets a BOYZ unit from your army that contains 10 or more models, models in that unit have a 5+ invulnerable save against that attack.（v1.2勘误后文本）

中文速查：
- 全军 BOYZ（暴徒小子）单位被攻击时全员6+无敌豁免
- 若该单位10人以上：升级为5+无敌豁免（以被攻击时的模型数为准，FAQ确认攻击中途掉到10人以下不降档）
