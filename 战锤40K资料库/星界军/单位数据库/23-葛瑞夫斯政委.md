### 领主政委 Lord Commissar（原文）
【原】"This model can be attached to the same units as a Commissar."
【译】本模型可以按普通政委的进驻规则加入单位。

### 坟墓漏斗 Funnel Them Into The Grave（原文）
【原】"Attacks made with this model's weapons have the [PRECISION] ability."
【译】本模型武器的攻击均带**[精准]**——点名单兵角色杀手。

### 以身作则 Lead By Example（原文）
【原】"Once per battle round, this unit can be affected by a friendly COMMISSAR's Summary Execution ability as if it were a friendly ASTRA MILITARUM unit."
【译】每战斗轮一次，本单位可被己方政委的"处决"能力影响（视作己方星界军单位）——自单位砍一个模型换不崩溃。

### 尽忠之终 Duty's End（原文）
【原】"Each time this model is destroyed, roll one D6: on a 4+, the attacking unit suffers 4 mortal wounds."
【译】本模型被摧毁时掷D6：4+→攻击方单位吃**4致命伤**。
→死亡陷阱：对面必须掂量点杀他的代价。
