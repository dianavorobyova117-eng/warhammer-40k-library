【原】"Once per turn, in your opponent's Shooting phase, when an enemy unit makes a ranged attack that targets a friendly REGIMENT unit within 3" of this model, after that enemy unit has shot, this model can shoot as if it were your Shooting phase, but it must target only that enemy unit."

【原】"In your Shooting phase, after this model has shot, if it is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, it can make a Normal move. If it does, until the end of the turn, this model is not eligible to declare a charge."
