## 能力

### 核心能力
- **深击（Deep Strike）**：可走增援入场，从战场外部署至任意位置（与所有敌军水平距离>8寸※，以11版核心规则原文为准待核对）——即旧版"曼塔突击"在11版并入核心能力，战斗服族全员自带。
- **领袖（Leader）**：可进驻上列危机单位。

### 为了至高善 For the Greater Good（阵营能力·全军通用）
【原】"If your Army Faction is T'AU EMPIRE, at the start of your Shooting phase, you can select units from your army with this ability to become Observer units."
【原】"During your Shooting phase, for each Observer unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase (excluding FORTIFICATION and Battle-shocked units) and is eligible to shoot, you can select one enemy unit that is visible to them to be marked as their Spotted unit until the end of the phase. Each enemy unit can only be marked as a Spotted unit once per phase."
【原】"Until the end of the phase, while a unit from your army with this ability (excluding Observer units) is targeting one or more Spotted units, it is a Guided unit, and each time a model from your army in a Guided unit makes an attack that targets a Spotted unit, improve the Ballistic Skill characteristic of that attack by 1, and, if the Spotted unit was marked by an Observer unit that has the Markerlight keyword, that attack has the [IGNORES COVER] ability."
→ 中文速记：射击阶段开始选**观察者**→观察者开火前指定可见敌军为其**标定目标**（同一敌军每阶段只能被标1次）→其余单位打标定目标时即**导引单位**：命中技能+1；若标定者带**标记（MARKERLIGHT）**关键词再加**无视掩体**。

### 短刃之道 Way of the Short Blade（原文）
【原】"While this model is leading a unit, each time a model in that unit makes an attack that targets an enemy unit within 9\", add 1 to the Wound roll."
本模型领衔单位中，模型对**9寸内**敌军发起攻击时造伤骰+1（远近战皆可）。

### 纯潮教诲 Puretide's Teachings（原文）
【原】"Once per battle round, you can target this model's unit with a Stratagem... reduce the CP cost of that use of that Stratagem by 1CP."
每战斗轮1次，对本单位使用战略时CP费用-1。

### 独立之力 Independent Power（原文）
【原】"If your army includes COMMANDER FARSIGHT, it cannot include any ETHEREAL units."
军队含远见则**不能含任何以太**（反之亦然）。

## 战术备注
- **2+命中/2+近战的近战怪物**：横扫8刀切小兵、劈击4刀S10切载具；配合短刃之道9寸内造伤+1，领衔的危机小队也全场+1造伤。
- 纯潮教诲＝每轮1次免费/半价战略，蒙特卡聚焦火力(1CP)给他带队是标配连招。
- 深击+12寸级移动：T2空降贴脸输出的节奏机器，但别忘了他是全队唯一不能改装备的指挥官。
- 军表注意事项：带远见就不能带以太/ Shadowsun体系，蒙特卡三指挥官阵容不受影响。
