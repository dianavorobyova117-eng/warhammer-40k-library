## 能力

### 死翼大导师 Grand Master of the Deathwing（原文）
【原】"While this model is leading a unit, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, if a Critical Hit is scored, that attack has the [PRECISION] ability."
带队时全队攻击掷出**重击（Critical Hit）即附带【精准】**——爆矢/近战暴击可越过护卫直接点名角色。
→ 死翼骑士的mace暴击、终结者双列爆矢暴击全部变点名武器。

### 报应打击 Strikes of Retribution（原文）
【原】"Each time a melee attack is allocated to this model, after the attacking model's unit has finished making its attacks, roll one D6 (to a maximum of six D6 per attacking unit): for each 4+, the attacking unit suffers 1 mortal wound."
每次近战攻击分配到本模型后，攻击单位打完时掷D6（每个攻击单位至多6粒）：**每粒4+该攻击单位吃1致命伤**。
→ 反近战威慑：敌人用大编队围殴他，最多白挨6个致命伤。
