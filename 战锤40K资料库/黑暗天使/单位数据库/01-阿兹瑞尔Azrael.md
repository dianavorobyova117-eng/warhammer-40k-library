## 能力

### 至高大导师 Supreme Grand Master（原文）
【原】"While this model is leading a unit, weapons equipped by models in that unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability."
带队时全单位武器获得**持续命中1**——黑天使第一领袖光环。
→ 常驻内环伙友/仲裁者砖：全员爆矢枪变相+命中。

### 精妙战术家 Masterful Tactician（原文）
【原】"At the start of your Command phase, if this model is on the battlefield, you gain 1CP."
你的指挥阶段开始时，若在战场，**白得1CP**——140分实际净成本更低。

### 雄狮之盔 The Lion Helm（原文）
【原】"Models in the bearer's unit have a 4+ invulnerable save. In addition, once per battle, in any phase, the bearer can summon a Watcher in the Dark. When it does, until the end of the phase, models in the bearer's unit have the Feel No Pain 4+ ability against mortal wounds."
**全队4+特救**＋每场1次召唤黑暗守望者（本阶段全队对致命伤4+痛觉无效）。
→ 本队自带4++，与巨石之怒"恪尽坚韧"（S>T减造伤）叠加成顶级坦砖。

### 核心能力
领袖（Leader）；阵营能力：**时刻之誓 Oath of Moment**（全军规则：指挥阶段选敌军1单位为誓约目标，攻击它可重掷命中）。
