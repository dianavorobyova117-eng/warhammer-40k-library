## 能力

### 阿斯塔特军旗 Astartes Banner（原文）
【原】"While this model is leading a unit, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in that unit."
带队时全队**OC+1**（多面军旗可叠加，官方FAQ确认）。

### 高擎军旗 Keep the Banner High（原文）
【原】"While this model is leading a unit, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, add 1 to the Hit roll if that unit is below its Starting Strength, and add 1 to the Wound roll as well if that unit is Below Half-strength."
带队时：本队**低于初始规模**→攻击命中+1；**低于半数**→造伤也+1。
→越打越强的死翼：5人死翼终结者死剩2人时命中+1造伤+1狂怒输出。
