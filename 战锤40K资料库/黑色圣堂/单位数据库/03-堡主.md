## 能力

### 激励典范 Inspirational Exemplar
【原】"While this model is leading a unit, each time a model in that unit makes a melee attack, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ scores a Critical Hit."
带领单位时：该单位近战攻击的**未修正命中骰5+即算重击（Critical Hit）**。
→重击联动致命命中/持续命中类武器技能：链剑(持续命中1)、大师级动力武器(致命命中)、雷霆锤(毁灭性伤口)全部提前触发。

### 虔诚狂热 Pious Fervour
【原】"Each time this model's unit is selected to fight, until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of this model's master-crafted power weapon for each enemy unit within 6" of this model (to a maximum of +3)."
本单位被选中战斗时：至阶段结束，本模型的大师级动力武器**每有一个6寸内敌军单位+1攻（上限+3）**——被围时7攻起。
