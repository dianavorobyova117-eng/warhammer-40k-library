### 致命崩毁 Deadly Demise D6

### 紧急战斗登车 Emergency Combat Embarkation
【原】"Once per turn, in your opponent's Charge phase, after an enemy unit has selected targets for its charge but before it makes a Charge move, you can select one ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that was selected as a target of that charge. Provided that unit is not within Engagement Range... and every model in that unit is within 3" of this TRANSPORT, it can embark within this TRANSPORT. The charging unit can then select new targets for its charge."
每轮1次，敌方冲锋阶段在冲锋宣布后移动前：被选为冲锋目标的己方阿斯塔特单位（未接战且全员距本车3寸内）可立即登车，敌方冲锋单位须重选冲锋目标。

### 受损 Damaged（1-5W）
剩余1-5生命时所有攻击命中-1。
