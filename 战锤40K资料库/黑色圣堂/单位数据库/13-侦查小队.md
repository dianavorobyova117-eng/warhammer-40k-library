### 游击战术 Guerrilla Tactics
【原】"At the end of your opponent's turn, if this unit is more than 6" away from all enemy models, you can remove this unit from the battlefield and place it into Strategic Reserves."
对手回合结束时：若本队距所有敌方模型超过6寸，可将其移入**战略预备役**（下轮从边线回来）。
