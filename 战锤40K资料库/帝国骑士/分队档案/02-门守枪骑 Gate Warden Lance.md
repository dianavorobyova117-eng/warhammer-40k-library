## 分队规则（团策）
### Dauntless Defenders 无畏守卫者
【原】At the start of the first battle round, select two objectives to be your foundations. Place a circular 40mm foundation marker in each one. When you draw a line from any part of one of your foundation markers to any part of the other, if any part of a model’s base (or any part of a model’s hull, for a model without a base) crosses that line, that model’s unit is said to be on your defensive line.
While an IMPERIAL KNIGHTS unit from your army is on your defensive line, that unit has the following ability:
Against the Horde: Each time a model in this unit makes an attack that targets a visible enemy unit, you can ignore any or all modifiers to the Hit roll, and weapons equipped by models in this unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability.

中文速查（逐条）：
- 第一战斗回合开始时：选两个目标点作为"基石"（foundation），各放一个40mm圆形基石标记
- 两基石之间拉一条直线=你的"防线"（defensive line），模型底盘/车体任何部分压线即视为"在防线上"
- 帝国骑士单位在防线上时获得 Against the Horde：攻击可见敌单位时忽略全部命中修正（无视-1掩体/重型等），且武器获得[SUSTAINED HITS 1]

EFFECT: Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in your unit have the [SUSTAINED HITS 2] ability.

## 组建限制（若有）
无额外限制；但全部增益依赖"防线"站位（两目标点连线），部署与目标点选择即为隐形限制。

## 战术画像
- 中线/半场防守反击流：Dominus级（Castellan/Valiant）+ Crusader/Defender是官方叙事钦定组合
- 团策自带"无视命中修正+SUSTAINED HITS 1"，站桩输出天花板，专克闪避/掩体流
- 目标倾向：固定目标点任务（基石尽量选两个相距近、能锁住中场的点，把防线变成火力走廊）
- 弱点：离开防线增益全失，怕被拉扯调动；MARSHAL THE DEFENCE+3"用于补位
